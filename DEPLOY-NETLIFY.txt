# ORCA — Netlify deploy notes

1. Netlify → Add new site → Deploy manually is NOT for this zip: this is SOURCE, built by Netlify.
   Use: Add new site → Import an existing project → drag this folder / connect the repo,
   or: unzip, `netlify init`, `netlify deploy --build --prod` (Netlify CLI).
   netlify.toml already sets: build = `bun install && bun run build`, plugin @netlify/plugin-nextjs.

2. Site settings → Environment variables → add ALL of these (values from your local .env):

   OPENROUTER_API_KEY=<paste your value>
   GROQ_API_KEY=<paste your value>
   ORCA_PLANNER_MODEL_ID=qwen/qwen3.8-27b
   ORCA_RESPONSE_MODEL_ID=qwen/qwen3.8-27b
   ORCA_SYNTHESIS_MODEL_ID=openai/gpt-oss-120b
   ORCA_FALLBACK_MODEL_ID=openai/gpt-oss-20b
   ORCA_OPENROUTER_URL=https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions
   ORCA_GROQ_URL=https://api.groq.com/openai/v1/chat/completions
   ORCA_RESPONSE_VISION_ENABLED=true
   INCOIS_API_KEY=<paste your value>
   INCOIS_BASE_URL=https://incois.gov.in/portal/datainfo
   INCOIS_PFZ_API_URL=https://incois.gov.in/portal/osf/map.jsp
   INCOIS_PFZ_WMS_URL=https://incois.gov.in/geoserver/wms
   COPERNICUS_USERNAME=your_copernicus_username
   COPERNICUS_PASSWORD=<paste your value>
   COPERNICUS_API_URL=https://data.marine.copernicus.eu/api
   IMD_API_KEY=<paste your value>
   IMD_API_BASE_URL=https://api.imd.gov.in
   IMD_API_VERSION=v1
   OPENWEATHER_API_KEY=<paste your value>
   NOAA_API_KEY=<paste your value>
   ORCA_INCOIS_ADAPTER_URL=/api/adapters/incois
   ORCA_INCOIS_ADAPTER_TOKEN=<paste your value>
   ORCA_COPERNICUS_ADAPTER_URL=/api/adapters/copernicus
   ORCA_COPERNICUS_ADAPTER_TOKEN=<paste your value>
   ORCA_WEATHER_ADAPTER_URL=/api/adapters/weather
   ORCA_WEATHER_ADAPTER_TOKEN=<paste your value>
   RISK_ENGINE_URL=/api/engines/risk
   RISK_ENGINE_TOKEN=<paste your value>
   GEOSPATIAL_ENGINE_URL=/api/engines/geospatial
   GEOSPATIAL_ENGINE_TOKEN=<paste your value>
   ROUTE_ENGINE_URL=/api/engines/route
   ROUTE_ENGINE_TOKEN=<paste your value>
   ORCA_TIMEOUT_MS=12000
   ORCA_TOTAL_TIMEOUT_MS=90000
   ORCA_RETRIES=1
   ORCA_MAX_DATA_AGE_MS=3600000
   ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=<paste your value>

   Notes:
   - ORCA_*_ADAPTER_URL and *_ENGINE_URL are /api/... paths on purpose: they resolve to this same
     deployment. Do NOT put localhost here (it would be auto-repointed anyway, but be explicit).
   - GROQ_API_KEY is required. ELEVENLABS_API_KEY is optional (realistic Hindi/Gujarati/English voice);
     without it English TTS uses Groq Orpheus (accept its terms once in the Groq console) or the browser.
   - OPENROUTER_API_KEY is no longer used by the pipeline; safe to omit.

3. After deploy: open the site → status button (top bar) → "Check connection" → expect 7 green rows.
   Or: curl https://<site>/api/orca?probe=1

4. Replay the intro any time with ?intro=force ; skip it with ?intro=off .
